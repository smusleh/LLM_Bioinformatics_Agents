# Estrogen-responsive genes in this dataset: STC2, LRIG1, KDM4B
Tags: STC2, LRIG1, KDM4B, estrogen-responsive, ER target, luminal, mRNA

- **STC2 (stanniocalcin 2)**: an estrogen-induced gene; high expression associates with ER+ tumours and favourable outcome.
- **LRIG1**: an estrogen-induced negative regulator of ERBB receptor signalling; expression is higher in ER+ luminal tumours and lower in ERBB2-amplified/basal tumours.
- **KDM4B (JMJD2B)**: a histone demethylase that is an ER-alpha target and coactivator, highly expressed in ER+ breast cancer.

Expected pattern: higher in Luminal A than in Basal-like or HER2-enriched tumours.

Source: Bouras et al., Cancer Res 2002 (STC2); Krig et al., Oncogene 2011 (LRIG1); Yang et al., Cancer Res 2010 (KDM4B).
