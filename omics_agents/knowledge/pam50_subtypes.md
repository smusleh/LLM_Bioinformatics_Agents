# PAM50 intrinsic subtypes of breast cancer
Tags: subtype, PAM50, Luminal A, LumA, Her2-enriched, Basal-like, classification

Breast tumours are grouped into intrinsic molecular subtypes from gene expression (Perou et al., Nature 2000; PAM50 classifier, Parker et al., J Clin Oncol 2009).
- **Luminal A (LumA)**: hormone-receptor positive (ER+/PR+), high expression of luminal/estrogen-responsive genes (ESR1, GATA3, FOXA1), low proliferation, best prognosis.
- **HER2-enriched (Her2)**: high expression of ERBB2 and genes of the 17q12 amplicon, often ER-low, high proliferation; responsive to HER2-targeted therapy.
- **Basal-like (Basal)**: mostly ER-/PR-/HER2- ("triple negative"), high proliferation, basal cytokeratins (KRT5/14/17), frequent TP53 mutation; poorer prognosis.

The TCGA breast study (Cancer Genome Atlas Network, Nature 2012) showed these subtypes are consistent across mRNA, miRNA, DNA methylation and RPPA protein data.

Source: Perou 2000; Parker 2009; TCGA Network 2012 (doi:10.1038/nature11412).
