# miR-30 family (miR-30a, miR-30e ...)
Tags: hsa-mir-30a, hsa-mir-30e, miR-30, tumour suppressor, luminal, miRNA, EMT

The miR-30 family (miR-30a-e) is generally regarded as tumour-suppressive in breast cancer: it maintains epithelial identity and restrains EMT and self-renewal (targets include UBC9, ITGB3 and vimentin).
- Reduced miR-30 expression has been associated with more aggressive, ER-negative tumours; higher levels associate with luminal/ER+ tumours.

Source: Yu et al., Oncogene 2010; Ouzounova et al., BMC Genomics 2013.
