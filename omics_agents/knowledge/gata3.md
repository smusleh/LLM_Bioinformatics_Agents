# GATA3
Tags: GATA3, luminal, transcription factor, protein, ER

GATA3 is a zinc-finger transcription factor required for luminal mammary epithelial differentiation. It is co-expressed with ER-alpha and participates in the ER transcriptional program together with FOXA1.
- High in Luminal tumours, low in Basal-like tumours; GATA3 is among the most frequently mutated genes in luminal breast cancer (TCGA 2012).

Source: Kouros-Mehr et al., Cell 2006; TCGA Network 2012.
