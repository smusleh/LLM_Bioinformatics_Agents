# Androgen receptor (AR)
Tags: AR, androgen receptor, protein, luminal androgen receptor, LAR, triple negative

AR is a steroid hormone receptor expressed in most ER+ breast tumours. Most Basal-like/triple-negative tumours are AR-low, except the "luminal androgen receptor" (LAR) subgroup of TNBC described by Lehmann et al. (J Clin Invest 2011).
- Expected pattern: higher in Luminal A than Basal-like tumours.

Source: Lehmann et al. 2011; TCGA Network 2012.
