# miR-17-92 cluster and paralogs (miR-17, miR-20a, miR-19b, miR-92a, miR-106a, miR-106b)
Tags: hsa-mir-17, hsa-mir-20a, hsa-mir-19b-2, hsa-mir-92a-1, hsa-mir-92a-2, hsa-mir-106a, hsa-mir-106b, oncomiR, MYC, miRNA

The miR-17-92 polycistron (MIR17HG, "oncomiR-1") encodes miR-17, -18a, -19a, -20a, -19b-1 and -92a-1. Paralogous clusters are miR-106a-363 (chrX) and miR-106b-25 (chr7).
- Transcriptionally activated by MYC; promotes proliferation and suppresses apoptosis (targets include PTEN, E2F1, BIM).
- In breast cancer, members of these clusters are expressed at higher levels in Basal-like / triple-negative tumours than in luminal tumours.

Source: He et al., Nature 2005; Mogilyansky & Rigoutsos, Cell Death Differ 2013; TCGA Network 2012.
