# let-7 family (let-7a, let-7b, let-7c ...)
Tags: hsa-let-7a-1, hsa-let-7b, hsa-let-7c, let-7, tumour suppressor, RAS, HMGA2, LIN28, miRNA

let-7 miRNAs are tumour-suppressive: they repress RAS, HMGA2 and MYC and are inhibited by LIN28. Low let-7 is associated with breast tumour-initiating cells and more aggressive disease.

Source: Yu et al., Cell 2007 (let-7 and breast tumour-initiating cells).
