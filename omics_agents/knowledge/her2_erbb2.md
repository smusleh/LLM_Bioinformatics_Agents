# HER2 (ERBB2)
Tags: HER2, ERBB2, HER2_pY1248, amplification, Her2-enriched, receptor tyrosine kinase, HER3

HER2 is a receptor tyrosine kinase of the EGFR family, encoded by ERBB2 on chromosome 17q12. Amplification causes very high HER2 protein and constitutive signalling (PI3K/AKT, MAPK).
- HER2 and phospho-HER2 (HER2_pY1248, RPPA) are highest in HER2-enriched tumours, compared with Basal-like and Luminal A.
- Targeted by trastuzumab, pertuzumab, trastuzumab deruxtecan and lapatinib.

Source: Slamon et al., Science 1987; TCGA Network 2012.
