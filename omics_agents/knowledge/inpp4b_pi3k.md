# INPP4B and the PI3K/AKT pathway
Tags: INPP4B, PTEN, PI3K, Akt, Akt_pS473, mTOR, tumour suppressor, protein, basal

INPP4B is a lipid phosphatase that antagonises PI3K/AKT signalling. INPP4B loss is frequent in Basal-like / triple-negative breast tumours, whereas expression is maintained in ER+ luminal tumours.

Source: Gewinner et al., Cancer Cell 2009; Fedele et al., PNAS 2010.
