# Estrogen receptor alpha (ESR1 / ER-alpha) and progesterone receptor (PR / PGR)
Tags: ER-alpha, ESR1, PR, PGR, hormone receptor, luminal, protein, RPPA, ER-alpha_pS118

ER-alpha is a ligand-activated nuclear transcription factor that drives growth of hormone-dependent breast tumours. PR (PGR) is an estrogen-induced ER target gene; PR expression indicates a functional ER pathway.
- Both are high in Luminal A tumours and low/absent in Basal-like tumours (the defining feature of triple-negative disease). HER2-enriched tumours are frequently ER-low.
- ER-alpha_pS118 is a phosphorylated (activated) form of ER-alpha measured by RPPA.
- Clinically, ER/PR status guides endocrine therapy (tamoxifen, aromatase inhibitors).

Source: TCGA Network 2012; standard clinical pathology of breast cancer.
