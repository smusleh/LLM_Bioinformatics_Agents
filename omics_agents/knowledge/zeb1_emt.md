# ZEB1 and epithelial-mesenchymal transition (EMT)
Tags: ZEB1, EMT, E-Cadherin, N-Cadherin, claudin-low, mesenchymal, miR-200

ZEB1 is an EMT transcription factor that represses E-cadherin (CDH1) and is itself repressed by the miR-200 family. High ZEB1 / N-cadherin with low E-cadherin marks mesenchymal states, characteristic of the claudin-low subtype and some Basal-like tumours.

Source: Gregory et al., Nat Cell Biol 2008; Prat et al., Breast Cancer Res 2010.
