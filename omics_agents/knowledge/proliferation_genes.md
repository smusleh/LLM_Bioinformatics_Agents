# Proliferation / cell-cycle genes (CCNA2, ASPM, E2F1, Cyclin B1, CDK1, PCNA, Cyclin E1)
Tags: proliferation, cell cycle, CCNA2, ASPM, E2F1, Cyclin_B1, CDK1, PCNA, Cyclin_E1, mitosis

Proliferation-associated genes form a strong co-expression module in breast cancer and are a major component of PAM50 and other prognostic signatures (e.g. Oncotype DX, MammaPrint).
- CCNA2 (cyclin A2), CCNB1/Cyclin B1, CDK1: drive S-phase and mitosis. ASPM: mitotic spindle protein. E2F1: transcription factor controlling G1/S genes.
- Expected pattern: higher in Basal-like and HER2-enriched tumours than in Luminal A tumours (LumA has the lowest proliferation).
- Cyclin E1 (CCNE1) is particularly associated with Basal-like / TP53-mutant tumours.

Source: Parker et al. 2009; Whitfield et al., Nat Rev Cancer 2006; TCGA Network 2012.
